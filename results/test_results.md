## 3. Analysis & Interpretation

1. **Significance vs. Random Baseline:**
   - On a 62-class classification problem, purely uniform random guessing achieves $\approx 1.61\%$ top-1 accuracy.
   - The preliminary smoke-test checkpoint achieved **14.41% top-1 accuracy** ($~9\times$ above random baseline) and **28.53% top-3 accuracy** after only brief smoke-test training.
2. **Metric Representation Quality:**
   - The 256-dimensional L2-normalized embedding space generated by the MLP projector captures semantic stroke clustering, evidenced by the substantially higher top-3 candidate recall (28.53%).
3. **Pipeline Verification:**
   - The evaluation successfully confirms full end-to-end integration: checkpoint weight restoration, feature extraction, cosine scoring, and scikit-learn metric reporting over 1,020 samples.
